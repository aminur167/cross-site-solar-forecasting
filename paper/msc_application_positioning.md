## 3. Statement of Purpose (SOP) Paragraph Template

You can adapt this paragraph directly into your Statement of Purpose:

> *"During my undergraduate studies at Hajee Mohammad Danesh Science and Technology University (HSTU), I recognized that while machine learning models achieve high accuracy on benchmark datasets, their deployment in safety-critical renewable energy grids is hindered by data scarcity and uncalibrated uncertainty. To bridge this gap, I designed and conducted an individual research study titled 'Data-Efficient and Uncertainty-Calibrated Cross-Site Solar PV Power Forecasting Under Temporal and Sensor-Availability Shifts' (currently under review at Elsevier’s Energy and AI). By coupling gradient boosted decision trees with Conformalized Quantile Regression (CQR), I formulated a few-shot adaptation pipeline that mitigates the commissioning bottleneck of newly built PV plants, reducing forecast error by 82.8% using merely one day of local observations while rigorously guaranteeing 92.95% prediction interval coverage on completely unseen solar assets. This experience solidified my technical proficiency in PyTorch, time-series domain adaptation, and distribution-free uncertainty estimation, fueling my ambition to pursue advanced graduate research in reliable AI for sustainable energy systems at [University Name]."*
