## Evidence-based project summary

A repository-level study evaluated retrospective transfer forecasting between two 50 kW PV sites in the same Romanian region using historical Open-Meteo weather estimates. In the local rerun, source-only LightGBM achieved 6.93% target daytime nMAE; one-day sample-weighted pooled adaptation achieved 6.97%, and target-only one-day training achieved 28.06%. Target-calibrated CQR empirical daytime coverage was 94.51% at a nominal 90% level. These are dataset-specific empirical results; temporal dependence and retrospective weather prevent claims of deployment guarantees or field robustness.
