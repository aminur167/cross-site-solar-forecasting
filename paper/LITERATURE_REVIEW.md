# Literature and Citation Audit

This file is an audit note, not a systematic literature review. Earlier taxonomies, paper summaries, comparison matrices, novelty claims, and author/journal statements were unsupported and have been withdrawn.

## Sources retained in the manuscript

- Romano, Patterson & Candès (2019), *Conformalized Quantile Regression*, NeurIPS 32: verified against the official proceedings; cited for the method, not as a guarantee under dependent time-series/site-shift conditions.
- Yang et al. (2018), *History and Trends in Solar Irradiance and PV Power Forecasting*, *Solar Energy* 168, 60–101: corrected bibliographic record (DOI 10.1016/j.solener.2017.11.023).
- Linca et al. (2026), *Two-Site Solar PV Inverter and Weather Dataset (Romania)*: local bundled `CITATION.cff` records authors, version, release date, repository, and CC BY 4.0.

## Removed errors

A former bibliography entry attributed DOI `10.3390/en16083450` to a PV dataset article; that DOI resolves to an unrelated reinforcement-learning powertrain-controls paper. The citation was removed and replaced with the source dataset's bundled citation metadata. A former Yang review entry mismatched the title and DOI and was replaced with the corrected 2018 review record. Two former cross-site/conformal entries could not be verified in this audit, so they and their dependent claims were removed.
