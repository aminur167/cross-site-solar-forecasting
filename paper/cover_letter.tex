\textbf{Research Context and Core Contributions:}\\
Grid integration of solar photovoltaic (PV) generation is frequently challenged by data scarcity at newly commissioned plants, telemetry dropouts at remote installations, and the lack of distribution-free uncertainty intervals to safeguard reserve scheduling. In this manuscript, we present a unified transfer forecasting framework coupling gradient boosted decision trees with Conformalized Quantile Regression (CQR).

Key scientific contributions reported in this work include:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Minimal Adaptation Frontier:} We demonstrate that training models from scratch on newly commissioned PV systems with limited telemetry ($k=1$ day) leads to severe overfitting ($\text{nMAE} = 29.33\%$), whereas our transfer adaptation framework delivers an immediate $82.8\%$ error reduction ($\text{nMAE} = 5.05\%$, Skill Score $=+0.4164$).
    \item \textbf{Calibrated Uncertainty Guarantees:} Using Conformalized Quantile Regression, the framework achieves an empirical Prediction Interval Coverage Probability (PICP) of $92.95\%$ on unseen target plants against a nominal $90\%$ target.
    \item \textbf{Sensor-Availability Resilience:} Through systematic stress-tests, we demonstrate that the model withstands up to $30\%$ random weather sensor dropout with negligible performance penalty ($+0.25\%$ nMAE), while identifying pyranometer loss as the primary failure mode.
    \item \textbf{SHAP Model Attribution:} We provide comprehensive game-theoretic attribution to validate the physical dynamics of the learned transfer models.
\end{enumerate}

This manuscript has not been published previously and is not under consideration for publication elsewhere.
